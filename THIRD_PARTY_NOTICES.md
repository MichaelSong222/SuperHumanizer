# Third party notices

SuperHumanizer adapts editorial ideas from the following MIT projects and retains their notices. The integrated instructions are self-contained and do not require installing those projects. They apply preservation and explicit-invocation constraints where upstream advice differs.

The plain-writing approach also follows the installed Writing Clearly and Concisely guide based on William Strunk Jr.’s 1918 Elements of Style. No guide text or student document is redistributed from that source.

The local humanizer_editor workflow and public examples were assembled for this package. No private agent configuration, student identity, document, account metadata, or detector report is included.
